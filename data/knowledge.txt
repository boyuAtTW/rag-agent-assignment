PROJECT TITAN: DEVELOPMENT SPECIFICATIONS
Date: February 3, 2026
Status: Internal Draft

### 1. Overview
Project Titan is our upcoming framework for decentralized AI model training. It aims to reduce GPU latency by 40% using a proprietary "Shard-Sync" protocol.

### 2. Architecture Decisions
We have decided to move away from PostgreSQL for our primary vector storage. Starting in Q2, all AI-related metadata will be stored in Qdrant (distributed mode). This change was driven by the need for sub-50ms retrieval times during peak loads.

### 4. Technical Hurdles
During the January stress test, the system experienced a "Memory Leak" when processing datasets larger than 5TB. Sarah suggests implementing a "Buffer-Flush" mechanism to prevent the Node.js workers from crashing.
